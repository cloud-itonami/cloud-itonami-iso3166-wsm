# Operator guide — WSM

Portal: no dedicated e-procurement transactional portal was confirmed
operational this session -- the Ministry of Finance's own site
(`mof.gov.ws`) publishes "Tender Advertisements" and "Tender Awards"
directly, and public procurement is administered by the Government
Tenders Board (Public Finance Management Act 2001 s.88) per Treasury
Instructions Section 6 Part K and its own B4 Schedule multi-tier
authority ladder (Works / Goods and General Services / Consultancy
Services, each from a Relevant Chief Executive Officer tier up through
a Cabinet tier above SAT 500,000). Entity: Ministry of Commerce,
Industry and Labour (MCIL) Companies Registry registration (Companies
Act 2001) plus Ministry of Customs and Revenue Business Licence (which
also bundles TIN issuance). Foreign-owned engagements seeking a Foreign
Investment Certificate (Foreign Investment Act 2000, as amended by the
Foreign Investment Amendment Act 2011) must clear the Act's own
Reserved/Restricted Activities regime (ss.3(1)/4(1)) -- a Reserved-List
activity is barred outright for a foreign investor; a Restricted-List
activity requires that activity's own itemized condition (an equity
ceiling or ratio, or a joint-venture-with-a-local-partner requirement)
to be independently satisfied. Government-supply engagements claiming
a procurement-authority tier must independently clear the B4 Schedule's
own tier for their declared category/contract value -- claiming a
lower tier than the schedule requires is a HARD hold. Human-gated
filing only.
