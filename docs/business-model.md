# Business model — WSM

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for the Independent
State of Samoa.

- Government Tenders Board, established by s.88 of the Public Finance
  Management Act 2001, approves public procurement per the Ministry of
  Finance's own Treasury Instructions Section 6 "Procurement &
  Contracting" (Part K, amended April 2020) and its own "Financial
  Delegation Threshold (B4 Schedule)" (see `src/marketentry/
  facts.cljc`) -- a genuinely MULTI-TIER ladder across three
  procurement-category tracks (Works; Goods and General Services;
  Consultancy Services), each from a Relevant Chief Executive Officer
  tier up through a Cabinet tier above SAT 500,000; no free-standing
  Public Procurement Act/Regulations was found
- Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL) Companies Registry
  (businessregistries.gov.ws) business/company registration under the
  Companies Act 2001, plus MCIL's own Foreign Investment Certificate
  regime under the Foreign Investment Act 2000 (as amended by the
  Foreign Investment Amendment Act 2011) -- a Reserved Activities List
  (6 activities reserved for Samoan citizens only) and a Restricted
  Activities List (3 itemized activities with heterogeneous conditions,
  including a 40%-foreign-equity CEILING for fishing -- the opposite
  direction from this family's Tonga catalog, whose own Restricted
  List sets MINIMUM equity floors)
- Ministry of Customs and Revenue Business Licence (Business License
  Act 1998 s.5 -- own text applies to any foreigner contracted by a
  government ministry/agency to supply goods or services in Samoa),
  Tax Identification Number (TIN, automatically issued upon Business
  Licence issuance -- not a separate registration step) and VAGST
  (Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 1993, 15%, SAT$130,000
  registration threshold) registration
- B4 Schedule procurement-authority-tier gate -- bars a filing that
  claims a lower-scrutiny approving authority (e.g. Relevant Chief
  Executive Officer) suffices when its own declared procurement
  category and contract value actually require a higher tier (Tenders
  Board or Cabinet)
